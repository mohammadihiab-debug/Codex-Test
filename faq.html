A LITTLE HELP?

Can’t find what you need? Tell us a little about your question and we’ll help you take the next step.

Contact usTrack an existing order
How do I choose my size?+

Check the size options and guidance on the product page. If you are between sizes or want a particular fit, contact us with the product name before ordering. Only choose a size that is shown as available.

How do I place an order?+

Open a product, choose an available size and quantity, and add it to your bag. Review the bag, continue to checkout and enter your delivery details. A successful order creates an order reference. Keep that reference so you can check its status.

Can I pay cash on delivery?+

Cash on delivery is the supported method when live checkout is enabled. The final payable amount appears before you place the order. No card details, mobile banking PIN or one-time password are required for a cash on delivery order.

Where do you deliver?+

The store is intended to deliver within Bangladesh, subject to courier coverage. Select the correct delivery area and enter your full address at checkout. Contact the store if you need to confirm service to your location.

How much does delivery cost?+

The delivery charge depends on the selected delivery area and any eligible free delivery offer. Checkout shows the delivery charge and total before you submit an order. See the delivery page for details.

When will my order arrive?+

The store must confirm dispatch and delivery estimates for your order. A reference means your order has been recorded, not that the parcel has shipped. Courier availability, holidays and the delivery location can affect timing.

How do I track my order?+

Visit Track your order and enter your order reference and the phone number you used at checkout. Once a parcel is dispatched, courier details can be added to your order.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

Contact the store with your reference as soon as possible. Before dispatch, changes may still be possible. An order already handed to the courier may need a different solution.

Can I return or exchange an item?+

The store needs to confirm its final exchange window, eligibility and charges before launch. Read the returns and exchange policy and contact the store before sending an item back. Keep the original labels, packaging and proof of purchase.

What if I receive the wrong or a damaged item?+

Contact us promptly with the order reference, the product name and a description of the problem. Keep the parcel packaging and include clear photos when you contact the team. The store will review the issue and confirm the next step.

Do I need an account?+

No account is needed to browse, save favorites or place an order. Your bag and wishlist are saved on the browser you use. Clearing browser storage or switching devices will not carry those preferences over.

How can I contact the store?+

Visit the contact page for the configured email, phone and business details. Include your order reference for order questions. The contact form prepares an email for you to send from your own email app.
